Research Privacy Notice

Status: validation prototype, not a production privacy policy.

Local processing

In this version, an optional wireless-bill PDF is processed in your browser. The site does not upload the PDF to a DealKeeper server.

Anonymous research record

If you choose to export a research record, it is designed to contain only carrier, promotion amount, term, bill month, credit amounts, audit status, remaining promotional value and experiment price variant. It should not contain your name, phone number, address, account number, PDF or carrier password.

No credentials

Do not provide carrier usernames, passwords, Social Security numbers, payment card data or other credentials.

Third-party code

The prototype loads the open-source PDF.js library from a public CDN in order to read PDF text locally. The bill itself is supplied to the library inside your browser.

Research endpoint

Central submission is disabled in this build. Before any central collection is enabled, this notice must be updated to identify the operator, purpose, retention period, deletion route and actual data processors.
