WHY THIS EXISTS

Bill credits can last for years.

Promotions may be spread over 24–36 bills and can involve activation windows, catch-up credits and eligibility conditions. DealKeeper is testing whether an independent, simple reconciliation layer is useful enough for consumers to pay for.
