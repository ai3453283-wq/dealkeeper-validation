WHY THIS EXISTS

Bill credits can last for years.

Promotions may be spread over 24–36 bills and can involve activation windows, catch-up credits and eligibility conditions. DealKeeper is testing both whether real promotions can be reconciled reliably and whether people understand and value ongoing monitoring before they ever need it.
